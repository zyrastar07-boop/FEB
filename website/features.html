Powerful Features

Every feature is designed to give you the best streaming experience possible.

Multi-Server Streaming

Never deal with dead links again. FEB automatically tests multiple streaming servers and picks the fastest, most reliable one for your content.

  • 9+ streaming servers integrated
  • Automatic server health checking
  • One-tap server switching
  • Fast failover on connection loss

HD & 4K Downloads

Download your favorite movies and TV shows in stunning quality. Our advanced HLS download system ensures you get the best possible file with true pause and resume support.

  • Multiple quality options (360p to 4K)
  • True pause and resume downloads
  • Download entire seasons at once
  • Built-in subtitle download

Smart Playback

Our intelligent playback system learns how you watch. Skip intros automatically, resume exactly where you left off, and enjoy buttery-smooth streaming.

  • Auto skip intro and outro
  • Remember position across devices
  • Auto quality adjustment
  • Cellular data saver mode

Episode Navigator

Never lose your place in a series. Our intuitive episode selector lets you browse seasons, see episode details, and jump to any episode instantly.

  • Season-by-season browsing
  • Episode thumbnails and synopses
  • Direct download from episode row
  • Auto-play next episode

Multi-Language Subtitles

Watch content in your preferred language. FEB supports multiple subtitle tracks and lets you customize appearance for optimal readability.

  • Multiple subtitle languages
  • Customizable size and color
  • Subtitle offset adjustment
  • Auto-download subtitles with videos
