Frequently Asked Questions

Is FEB free to use?

Yes, FEB is completely free to download and use. We believe everyone deserves access to great entertainment.

Is it safe to install APK files?

Yes, as long as you download from our official website. Our APK is signed and verified. We never require unnecessary permissions.

Why is the Android APK available before iOS?

Android's open ecosystem allows us to distribute the app directly. iOS requires App Store approval which takes additional time.

Will my downloads work offline?

Absolutely! Downloaded movies and episodes can be watched offline without an internet connection.

How often is FEB updated?

We push updates regularly to add new features, fix bugs, and improve streaming performance. Subscribe to our social channels for update announcements.
