About DMCA

The Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) provides a process for copyright owners to notify platforms about infringing content. Since FEB aggregates content from third-party sources, we act as a intermediary and will respond to valid DMCA takedown requests.

Submit a DMCA Notice

If you are a copyright owner or authorized agent and believe that content on or through FEB infringes your copyright, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice.

DMCA Takedown Request

Perjury is a crime. Filing a false DMCA notice may result in legal consequences.

Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, you may submit a counter-notification. Counter-notifications must include:

  • Your physical or electronic signature
  • Identification of the removed content and its location before removal
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the content was removed by mistake
  • Your name, address, and phone number
